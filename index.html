What we do

FRANK Autonomous Systems builds the autonomy layer for planetary rovers. Mission 1 puts the first autonomous swarm on the Moon: hazard mapping and site-characterisation surveys first, subsurface mineral prospecting second. Ground-truth data the lunar economy needs.

FRANK

Flexible Recurrent Active-memory Neural Kernel

Brain-inspired decision engine. Full rover autonomy in 20ms on a Raspberry Pi.

SAPPERS

Cheap, redundant rovers. $1M each vs $500M VIPER. Lose one, mission continues.

SUNBEAM

Solar tower base station. Charging, comms, thermal. Enables permanent operations.
