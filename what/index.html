What we do

Every meter a rover drives on the Moon is driven by a person on Earth, 2.6 seconds behind. That sets the cost of everything done on the lunar surface. We build the autonomy that removes the person. Mission 1 lands a 5.9 kg solar rover in 2028 and drives it with nobody in the loop.

FRANK

Flexible Recurrent Active-memory Neural Kernel

Brain-inspired decision engine. ~1M parameters, trained with reinforcement learning from scratch. Runs onboard on a Raspberry Pi.

SAPPERS

A 5.9 kg solar rover, $3.85M delivered to the surface. Small because the autonomy is small.

SUNBEAM

Solar tower base station. Charging, comms, thermal. Enables permanent operations.
