What we do

A rover on the Moon is driven by a team on Earth, a few metres at a time. That works for one rover. It does not work for ten. We build the autonomy that takes the team out of the loop: one command at the start, then the rover drives itself. Mission 1 launches in late 2028 to drive 1 km with zero ground commands.

FRANK

Flexible Recurrent Active-memory Neural Kernel

A learned policy of about 1M parameters. Perception, localisation, hazard detection and planning on a Raspberry Pi 5, on 58 mW.

DEMO-1

Twelve field runs in October 2026. 1,827 m autonomous, with two complete surveys without GPS, both at night.

ONE OPERATOR

One command per traverse. The rover does not need the link to drive, so one operator can hold a fleet.
