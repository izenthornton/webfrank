What we do

A rover on the Moon is driven by a team on Earth, a few metres at a time. That works for one rover. It does not work for ten. We build the autonomy that takes the team out of the loop: one command at the start, then the rover drives itself. Mission 1 launches in late 2028 to drive 1 km with zero ground commands.
