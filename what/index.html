What we do

NASA's VIPER rover is driven from a control room at Ames, 4.5 metres at a time.

That works for one rover. It does not work for ten.

Operations scale with the team.

FRANK is learned onboard autonomy for small lunar rovers. The operator sends one command per traverse and the rover drives the rest.

The surface does not scale

Every rover flown so far has been a one-off, with a control room to match.

A learned policy drives the rover from on board. One command at the start, then nothing. A deterministic safety layer can stop it; it fired once in the field and the rover stopped.

FRANK

Flexible Recurrent Active-memory Neural Kernel

Reinforcement learning from scratch, no pre-training. Sim-trained policy, real hardware, no teleoperation. Provisional patent filed.

The policy

FRANK does perception, localisation, hazard detection and planning. On a Raspberry Pi 5.

The safety layer

A deterministic proximity veto and a sensor-loss watchdog. They can stop FRANK; FRANK cannot stop them.
