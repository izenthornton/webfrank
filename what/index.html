What we do

Every meter a rover drives on the Moon is driven by a person on Earth, 2.6 seconds behind. That sets the cost of everything done on the lunar surface. We build the autonomy that removes the person. Mission 1 targets a late-2028 launch. It drives a 6 kg solar rover 1 km with zero ground commands.

FRANK

Flexible Recurrent Active-memory Neural Kernel

Brain-inspired decision engine. ~1M parameters, trained with reinforcement learning from scratch. Runs onboard on a Raspberry Pi 5.
